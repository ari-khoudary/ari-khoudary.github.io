In an utterly surreal turn of events, I have officially accepted an offer to begin as a [MindCORE postdoctoral fellow](https://mindcore.sas.upenn.edu/post-doctoral-research-fellowship/) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/) this fall. I'll collaborate with [Joe Kable](https://www.kablelab.com/), [Josh Gold](https://www.med.upenn.edu/goldlab/), and potentially several others to continue investigating how (episodic) memory supports flexible and adaptive decision-making in dynamic and uncertain environments.
